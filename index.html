What we do

We develop a scheduling application built around timeboxing: giving each task its own fixed block of time. Instead of a long to-do list that keeps growing, your day becomes a short series of clear, manageable blocks.

⏱️

Time you can see

Every task gets a visible block on your day, so it's easier to know what to do now and what comes next.

🎯

One thing at a time

Small, defined blocks help you start, stay with the task, and stop when the time is up.

🌱

Built for ADHD

We design with the needs of people with ADHD in mind, keeping planning simple and low-pressure.

Our product

A timebox-based scheduling app for people with ADHD, turning plans into time blocks you can actually follow.

  • Timebox your tasks — place each task into its own block of time.
  • See your whole day — a simple timeline instead of an overwhelming list.
  • Keep it simple — fewer decisions, less friction, more done.
